The wordmark is drawn from eleven hand-built glyph skeletons — no font is embedded or referenced. Bars, bowls and diagonals share one 22-unit weight, and the whole word is slanted 9° for momentum. Edit a glyph in tools/branding/build-branding.mjs and every variant follows.
